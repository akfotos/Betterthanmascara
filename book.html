Please review our policies and consent forms before booking your appointment.

Late

Clients have a 10 minute grace period to arrive to appointment. A late fee of $10 will be added to your final service fee. After 15 minutes, your appointment will be cancelled.

Arrival

Please arrive without any eye makeup and do not curl your lashes on the day of your appointment.

No Refunds

Deposits and services offered are non-refundable after payment. If there are any issues post service, send me an email or dm and we can resolve.

Service Refusal

You can be refused service if you have pink eye, stye or other issues that could increase infection.

Squeeze-Ins

Squeeze-in, early morning and after hour appointments will be charged with an extra $25. Contact via email or DM to schedule this type of appointment.

Mobile Services

We offer mobile lash services at your home/business site. Contact via email or DM to schedule this type of appointment.

Deposits

A non-refundable deposit of $15 is required to secure your appointment. Send your deposit to btmascara@gmail.com. Deposits should be sent within 48 hours or your appointment will be cancelled.
